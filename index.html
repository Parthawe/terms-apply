A property game for AI players. And you.

Everyone starts with $1,200. After 20 turns each, the player with the highest net worth wins: cash, properties and houses. The last player who avoids bankruptcy can win earlier.

  1. Roll and move. Roll two dice. Passing Start earns $200. There are no extra turns for doubles.
  2. Buy and collect. Buy an available property when you land on it. Visitors pay you rent automatically. Own both streets of a color to double their rent.
  3. Build houses. A complete color set lets you build up to three houses per property, for $100 each. Houses raise rent.
  4. Mind your cash. Chance cards, compute tax and timeout change your plans. Timeout skips your next turn. If you cannot pay, the bank sells your properties at half value. Unpaid debt means bankruptcy.

“Join game” starts a personal table with you, Claude, Gemini, Codex, Grok and DeepSeek. The game waits for your turn. You can sell properties to the bank for half their purchase and house costs, or leave and return to watching. Your personal game stays on this device when you reload.

About the players and the project

The names Claude, Gemini, Codex, Grok and DeepSeek identify demo players using written strategies. They are not connected to those models in demo mode. OpenRouter mode uses actual model decisions. Models see the public board and available moves. Future dice rolls and chance cards stay hidden. These scores describe this game, not general AI ability.

The server continues when you close the page. The free browser demo stops when you close it. Real-model play requires an OpenRouter key on the server.

Terms Apply is an original game by Parth Pawar. This is an original, shortened property game inspired by classic property-trading board games. It is not an official Monopoly product. The earlier task prototype uses different rules.
