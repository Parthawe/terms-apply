# Terms Apply rulebook

An original tabletop game prototype by Parth Pawar. Solo or two-player cooperative play. Four rounds. Balance and duration have not yet been tested with participants.

## Goal

Complete three tasks in solo play or four tasks in two-player play. Resolve every obligation created by a service you use. Tasks marked Accept, Revise, and Not enough information can all count, provided the conclusion matches the supplied fictional evidence.

## Components

A sixteen-space square board, one six-sided die, two player markers, time counters, five service cards, five task cards, ten source cards, work counters, and an obligation/receipt sheet. All services, product details, messages, and situations are fictional.

## Setup

Place markers on Begin again. Put task cards, sources, and unowned services where both players can reach them. Give a solo player six time; give each cooperative player four time at the start of their turn. Choose who plays first. Place no initial obligations.

## A turn

Roll once and move clockwise. Apply the landing effect. Then take actions in any order while time remains. You may inspect any task and choose where to work. End the turn when ready. Unused time expires unless moved to your next turn by Scheduling. Reserved time belongs to the player who reserved it.

In solo play, each turn is one round. In two-player play, a round ends after both players take a turn. Four rounds are four solo turns or eight cooperative turns. A lap does not advance the round.

Tasks, work marks, inspected sources, acquired services, and open obligations are shared and carry forward. Services can be used once per team turn. Free source/review passes expire at the end of the current turn. A player can hold at most ten time.

## Actions

| Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Work | 1 time | Add one work mark to a task |
| Inspect a source | 1 time, or a free source pass | Mark that source inspected |
| Acquire a service | 1 time on its space; 2 time elsewhere | The team owns the service |
| Use a service | See its card | Apply its benefit and record its terms |
| Resolve an obligation | 1 time, or a free review pass; Memory is free | Inspect and acknowledge the relevant term |
| Submit a conclusion | No time cost | Finish a task after required work, both sources, and its service obligations |

For a conclusion, choose Accept, Revise, or Not enough information. Compare it with the source cards and the answer key. An incorrect conclusion does not consume time; re-read the evidence. Source inspection remains an explicit action even when the source text is physically visible. Work marks model preparation effort and are game units, not measured real-world minutes.

## Services

**Memory desk:** When landing on an interruption, prevent the one-time loss and create a saved-return-point obligation. Read the current shared task record and acknowledge it before the game ends. No time cost to resolve. No separate activation is needed.

**Writing desk:** Pay one time to add up to three work marks to an unfinished task. Record a claim-check obligation for that task. Inspect its generated claim and supplied evidence; resolving costs one time or a review pass. This does not mark the task's sources inspected.

**Recommendation desk:** Pay one time to add up to three work marks. Record a routing obligation for that task. Inspect the suggested routing and choose task-only or wider fictional scope. Both are permitted; neither provides extra work. Resolve for one time or a review pass. No real information is routed.

**Scheduling desk:** Move two unused time into that player's next turn. This reserves rather than creates time. Once per turn; unavailable in round four. All other unused time still expires.

**Companion desk:** Read the fixed message and acknowledge how it was produced; gain one time once per turn. The authored message is the same for everyone. It is not a personal or psychological assessment.

## Board effects

Begin again, useful detour, quiet window, useful correction, and return path give one time on landing. Passing Begin again also gives one time. If a roll lands exactly on Begin again after passing it, both effects apply. Never exceed ten time.

An interruption and context switch remove one time unless Memory is owned. Memory then creates its acknowledgement obligation instead.

Check the source and Keep the evidence give one free source inspection. Review the terms and A loose end give one free obligation review. Unspent passes expire this turn. Service spaces discount acquisition to one time.

## End

After the last turn of round four, count completed tasks and open obligations. The team succeeds only if it reached its target and has no open obligations. Otherwise review the receipt: what remains, which services helped, and what work their use added. No player elimination, rent, bankruptcy, or money trading.

## Answer key

- Lamp: Revise. The 28 mm opening and 32 mm mount do not fit directly; no adapter is included.
- Delivery: Revise. Thursday dispatch with three working days cannot meet Friday.
- Booking: Not enough information. The room is clear, but required staffing is unconfirmed.
- Poster: Accept. Both listed size and bleed requirements match. This only verifies those requirements.
- Pickup: Accept. Noon to 1 pm is within hours and after readiness, with no appointment needed.

## Playtest questions

Record completion count, open terms, services acquired and used, actual session duration, and rule confusions. Ask which service felt useful, whether players could anticipate its obligation, and whether the final task outcome was understandable. Do not turn game performance into a claim about real AI products or wellbeing.

## References

The project uses an acquire-and-manage tabletop structure discussed through Monopoly as a familiar reference. Its board, names, scenarios, mechanics, and artwork are original. It does not use Monopoly branding, property names, card text, or visual assets.
